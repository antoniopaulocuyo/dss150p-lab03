## 4. Configuration Structure

* **`../config/settings.yml`**: Holds non-secret pipeline defaults such as paths and quality thresholds.
* **`../.env`**: Holds environment-specific and secret values such as database credentials and hosts.
* **`../src/config.py`**: Acts as the single source of truth that merges both sources into a `SETTINGS` dict and `DB` dict. The rest of `../src` imports exclusively from here, ensuring no individual module reads `../.env` or `settings.yml` directly.
